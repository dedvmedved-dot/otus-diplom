# TLS / CA model

TLS_CA_MODE is OWNER_INPUT_REQUIRED (depends on the S3 endpoint).

Options: PUBLIC_CA | PRIVATE_CA | CUSTOM_CA_BUNDLE

For private/custom CA, P7C-B would mount a CA bundle (ConfigMap/Secret) into
Velero/plugin without disabling TLS verification.

Strictly forbidden future design:
  insecureSkipTLSVerify, curl -k as production, TLS verification disable
