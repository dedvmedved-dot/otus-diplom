# Local snapshot/restore canary design (NOT executed in R1)

1 temp namespace
2 temp small PVC (accepted LINSTOR StorageClass piraeus-r2)
3 temp pod writes deterministic test data
4 VolumeSnapshot (class piraeus-r2-snapclass)
5 wait readyToUse=true
6 restore new PVC from VolumeSnapshot
7 read deterministic data; compare checksum
8 delete canary resources
9 prove no snapshot/LINSTOR residue

Future P7C-B1 must prove:
  CSI_SNAPSHOT_CREATE=PASS, CSI_SNAPSHOT_READY=PASS,
  PVC_RESTORE_FROM_SNAPSHOT=PASS, DATA_INTEGRITY=PASS, CLEANUP=PASS
