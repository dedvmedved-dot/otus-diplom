# AutoPlace PlaceCount semantics (v1.33.2, source-proven)

SelectFilter.PlaceCount (from linstor-csi volume.Parameters.PlacementCount) is
'number of replicas of the volume in total' (pkg/volume/parameter.go:85).

SelectionManager.getReplicaCount (autoplacer/SelectionManager.java:341):
  if selectFilter.replicaCount > alreadyExistingCount:
      ret = replicaCount - alreadyExistingCount
  => PlaceCount is TOTAL diskful replicas; existing diskful are counted;
     autoplace selects PlaceCount - existing additional replicas.

AUTOPLACE_PLACECOUNT_SEMANTICS=total desired diskful (existing counted)
EXISTING_RESOURCE_COUNTED=YES
SECOND_DISKFUL_REQUESTED=YES (2 - 1 = 1 additional)
SECOND_DISKFUL_CREATED=NO (observed: 'failed to autoplace constraint replicas',
  'autoplaced on 1 nodes' + TieBreaker + diskless)
IF_NO_WHY=autoplace constraint did not complete the additional diskful; instead a
  TieBreaker was auto-added and quorum switched to majority (see 04).
