# AutoQuorum / TieBreaker ordering (v1.33.2)

CtrlRscAutoQuorumHelper: auto-quorum activates 'majority' when quorum is feasible,
  else deactivates ("not enough resources for quorum").
CtrlRscAutoTieBreakerHelper: auto tie-breaker is added (auto-add-quorum-tiebreaker=True)
  when a tie-breaker is required by quorum (odd diskful count).

Observed order in the failure:
  1 autoplace -> 1 diskful (restore seed) + diskless(OnRemaining)
  2 auto tie-breaker added (node-03)
  3 AutoQuorum quorum off -> majority
  4 primary promotion attempt -> 'Need access to UpToDate data' (1 UpToDate < majority 2)
