# Replica count decision

Upstream default: replicas=2 (with --leader-election=true)

3-node HA control-plane, all control-plane tainted, snapshot-controller runs
with leader election; replicas=2 gives availability across single-node loss.

SNAPSHOT_CONTROLLER_REPLICAS_SELECTED=2
RATIONALE: upstream default; leader-election enabled; HA control-plane tolerance
to a single node failure. (replicas=1 noted in R1 was a misreading; upstream is 2.)
