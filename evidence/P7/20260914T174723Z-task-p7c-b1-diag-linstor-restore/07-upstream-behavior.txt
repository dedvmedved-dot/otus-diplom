# Upstream behavior comparison

NORMAL_CREATE_PLACEMENT: CreateVolume without content source -> autoplace places
  2 diskful replicas (placementCount=2) + diskless/tie-breaker as configured. WORKS.

SNAPSHOT_RESTORE_PLACEMENT: VolFromSnap restores snapshot onto ONE node first
  (reconcileSnapshotResources), then reconcileResourcePlacement autoplace. In the
  observed failure autoplace resulted in 1 diskful + tie-breaker, not 2 diskful.

DIFFERENCE_CAUSE: restore path seeds a single-node resource before autoplace; the
  subsequent autoplace does not reconcile to 2 UpToDate diskful replicas before DRBD
  AutoQuorum(majority) primary promotion.
